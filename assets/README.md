# AquaGuard AI

## Farm-Level Groundwater Impact & Irrigation Intelligence

AquaGuard is a prototype decision-support system designed to connect:

1. Crop-water demand
2. Irrigation requirement
3. Surface-water contribution
4. Estimated groundwater requirement
5. Irrigation scenarios
6. Potential groundwater savings

## Technology

- Python
- Streamlit
- SQLite
- Pandas

## Core architecture

Farm Inputs
↓
Water Calculation Engine
↓
Crop Water Requirement
↓
Irrigation Requirement
↓
Water Source Allocation
↓
Estimated Groundwater
↓
Scenario Comparison
↓
Potential Water Saving
